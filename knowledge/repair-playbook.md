# Repair Playbook - Steel and Concrete Assets

> SAMPLE FIXTURE for the InfraScan 3D demo. This is generic engineering guidance written for the
> prototype, not an authoritative standard. Replace with the client's real, controlled documents
> before production use.

## Corrosion - structural steel

Acceptance is driven by remaining wall or section thickness measured against the design
thickness on the material specification sheet. Spot measuring at the worst point of the mask
region is required before a repair is scoped.

- Surface rust with no section loss: wire-brush to St 2, recoat with the specified primer system.
- Light pitting under 10 percent section loss: blast clean, apply epoxy patch, add to 6-month monitoring.
- Pitting at or above 10 percent section loss, or any through-wall penetration: High severity.
  Isolate the section, engineering review, cut out and weld a repair sleeve.

## Cracking - concrete

Crack width is the deciding measurement. Compare against the width limit in the applicable
design code for the exposure class of the member.

- Hairline cracking below the code width limit, map-cracking only: seal with a penetrating sealer, re-survey annually.
- Cracking at or near the width limit: inject epoxy or polyurethane resin, verify with monthly surveys for 6 months.
- Cracking above the limit, or accompanied by rust staining and spalling: High severity. Restrict access,
  structural assessment, partial depth patch repair.

## Spalling / delamination - deck and facade

Tap survey or infragraphy confirms the sound/unsound boundary. Repair extent is normally cut back
until at least 25 mm of sound material surrounds the removal zone.

## Potholes - asphalt

Pothole depth decides the repair family: surface patch below 25 mm, full-depth patch at or above
25 mm, and deep structural patch when the base course is exposed.

## Priority mapping

Severity High or Critical, or any finding on a load-path member, maps to work-order priority P0/P1.
Medium maps to P2 with a scheduled window. Low maps to P3 monitoring only.
